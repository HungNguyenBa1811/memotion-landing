## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary:** The "Sandwich Generation" (adults aged 30–55 in Vietnam) balancing demanding careers with caring for aging parents, often living in separate cities (e.g., working in Hanoi/HCMC while parents are in provincial hometowns).
- **Secondary:** Elderly patients recovering from stroke, surgery, joint degeneration, or seeking to preserve independent mobility.
- **Tertiary:** Caregivers and physical therapy practitioners monitoring home recovery adherence.

## Product Purpose
Memotion bridges clinical rehabilitation and home care through an AI-powered assistant. It enables safe, medically validated physical therapy at home, giving seniors independence and giving their children absolute peace of mind without the prohibitive cost of private in-home nursing.

## Positioning
Unlike generic fitness apps or rigid telehealth video calls, Memotion provides closed-loop, real-time 3D posture recognition and audio guidance using proprietary clinical datasets (partnered with Military Traditional Medicine Institute - Viện YHCT Quân Đội), delivering hospital-grade therapy feedback at 1/20th the cost.

## Operating Context
- Used in Vietnamese households with varied lighting and space constraints.
- Dual-persona interface: Large, high-legibility, voice-prompted UI for seniors; rich telemetry dashboard with notifications for adult children.
- Multi-generational emotional connection: Shared progress milestones and peace of mind.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **4-Step AI Virtual Assistant:**
  1. Real-time Posture Recognition (Computer Vision).
  2. Peak Joint Angle Measurement (Clinical precision).
  3. Workout Stream Synchronization.
  4. Real-time Voice Guidance & Correction.
- **Gait Analysis & Fall Warning:** Early anomaly detection based on proprietary geriatric movement datasets.
- **Smart ML Ecosystem:** Personalized nutrition and low-impact activity recommendations.
- **Technical Stack:** React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS v3.4.17 (Legacy Config), Framer Motion, Lucide React.
- **Language:** Vietnamese (primary target market).

## Product Principles
1. **Dignity in Every Interaction:** Treat elderly users as autonomous individuals striving for strength, not passive patients.
2. **Clinical Rigor Meets Human Warmth:** Every AI feature must be anchored in verified medical protocol while feeling gentle and encouraging.
3. **Effortless Reassurance:** Give busy caregivers instant, unambiguous confidence that their parents are safe and progressing.
4. **Apple-Grade Clarity and Craft:** Spatial hierarchy, crisp typographic contrast, deliberate fluid physics, and tactile micro-interactions that elevate health-tech beyond generic clinical portals.
